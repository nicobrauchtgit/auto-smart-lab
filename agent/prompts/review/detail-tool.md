Inspect a fixed section of the selected experiment's evaluation evidence: curves (pooled ROC/PR points), folds (per-fold metrics and curves), or candidate (factory configuration and CV design). This tool reads the already captured evidence and runs no fits. Curve points are [false-positive rate, true-positive rate] for ROC and [recall, precision] for PR, downsampled to at most 101 points; AUC/AP use all scores.
