## Evidence labels

This task has no training targets. A development score exists only for
samples whose class someone has established. Where you can tell with
confidence that a sample is clearly positive or clearly negative, record it
with `evidence_label`, giving the method and the reason. Label only what you
are sure of: a wrong label misleads every later measurement, and the
validation expert reviews every positive you propose. A few certain labels are
worth more than many likely ones. Record in the findings journal how many you
labelled and by which methods, not which samples.
