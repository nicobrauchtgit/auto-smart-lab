## Evidence review

This task has no training targets; its development score rests on evidence
labels proposed by the data expert. Review every proposed positive yourself,
with your own method, and accept or reject it with `evidence_label`, giving
your reason. Only accepted labels count. In your verdict, say how the evidence
was found and what that means for the measured score: samples found by
inspection are the clearest cases, so the score on them is likely higher than
on the rest.
